Privacy Policy

This tool is operated for a single TikTok creator account and is not a public-facing service.

Data accessed:

  • Basic TikTok profile info (user.info.basic scope)
  • Video list and performance metrics for the connected account only (video.list scope)

Data storage:

  • Access/refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own server, encrypted at rest where supported, and are never shared with any third party.
  • Analytics data is stored locally in a private database for the sole purpose of informing the operator's own content decisions.

Data sharing:

  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs are used.

Data retention/deletion:

  • The operator may delete all stored data at any time.
  • Disconnecting the TikTok account revokes tool access immediately.
